## What this tab is for
Overview is the pulse of your account: what you spent, what you saved, how many requests went through the gateway and the general health — all at a glance, without opening every other tab.

## How to use it
It opens on its own when you sign in. The cards at the top summarise the period; the blocks below lead to the detail (click to jump to the matching tab). Use it to answer "is everything fine?" — the detail of "where did the money go" lives in Cost & Usage.

## Common questions
- **Why do the numbers differ from Cost & Usage?** Overview uses a fixed 30-day window; Cost & Usage lets you pick the period and granularity.
- **Nothing shows up.** You have not made any requests through the gateway yet. Set up an API key and point your app at the gateway.
