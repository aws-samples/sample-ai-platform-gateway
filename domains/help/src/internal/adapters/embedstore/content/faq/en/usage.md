## What this tab is for
Cost & Usage shows where the money went: cost, requests, tokens and latency, broken down by model, feature, team, app and provider — the unified view of every LLM you use through the gateway.

## How to use it
Pick the period and the granularity (hour/day). The donut charts split spend by dimension; the provider table details requests, tokens, cost and cache. To compare teams, use the team selector (it appears once you have more than one).

## Common questions
- **Savings do not show up here.** Savings live in the ROI & Savings tab — this tab is only what left your pocket, so the two are never mixed.
- **Latency looks low.** It counts only the synchronous path; batch calls are left out so they do not skew the average.
