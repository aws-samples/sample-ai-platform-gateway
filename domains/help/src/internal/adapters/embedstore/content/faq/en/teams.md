## What this tab is for
Teams & Apps is where you create and organise the hierarchy that separates your cost: **org → team → app**. Create teams and apps here; then point an API key at them. The org is your account (it comes from your login) and is not created here.

## How to use it
Use "New team" to create a team; inside the card, add apps, rename, archive or remove. The card is also where you set the team policy (budget and models) and, on Business, manage the team members. Renaming does not break history — the id is stable.

## Common questions
- **Do I need a team before a key?** Yes. Issuing a key only associates it with a team/app that already exists.
- **Archive vs remove?** Archiving preserves history and drops the team from new associations; removing is only possible when no apps or keys depend on it.
- **What is a team member for?** On Business the team is the access group: the member inherits the team's models and budget and (if they are a dev) is confined to that scope.
