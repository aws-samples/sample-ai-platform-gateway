## What this tab is for
Settings brings together your organisation's details, the gateway API key the console uses (Playground), the org members and account security.

## How to use it
Save the gateway API key here to enable the Playground. Under Members, invite people and set the role (owner/admin/billing/dev); on Business, access is organised by team. The org comes from your login and is read-only.

## Common questions
- **I cannot change my org.** The org comes from the token — it is your account. Only a platform operator crosses orgs.
- **What is the difference between a role and a team?** A role is what the person does (owner/admin/…); a team is the resource scope they reach (on Business).
